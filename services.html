What We Do


If you are facing an overwhelming project, or past attempts to organize your space have not worked, it may be time to consider working with a professional organizer.

Organize

  • Develop and implement a customized organization plan for any area of your home or office
  • Repurpose or reclaim a room, closet, or storage area for new use
  • Shop for organization products
  • Discard or donate items no longer needed

Home Sale Preparation

  • De-clutter and arrange belongings to maximize your homes appeal to buyers
  • Get your belongings settled after a move
  • Assist with downsizing

Optimize

  • Support you in improving your skills in organizing objects, systems, or time management
  • Maintain organization with quarterly, bi-annual, or annual visits

How It Works


We'll begin with an efficient and effective free phone or video call to discuss the basics or your space and your goals for organization. When you're ready we can schedule a time to visit your home or office and begin work.
The initial organizing session will begin with a tour of your space and questions about what is working, what is not working, and what the dream outcome is for your space.
Then we begin working! We remove all items, sort, eliminate, and put away neatly. I can work side by side with you or independently with intermittent input from you.
Sessions are scheduled in 4 hour block minimums with the final 20 minutes dedicated to cleaning up, reviewing the work we did, and planning the next steps.
4 hours can be enough to complete a small project such as an average closet. Larger projects are better suited to multiple sessions of 4 to 8 hours each.
