Organizing has been a lifelong passion of mine. Over the years, I’ve helped friends and family transform cluttered, overwhelming spaces into calm, functional ones; whether they were settling into a new home or just needing a fresh start.

I understand how the right systems can reduce stress, save time, and make everyday life run more smoothly. I bring that same practical experience and positive energy to every client session.

Before becoming a professional organizer, I worked as an elementary school teacher and raised two children, all while navigating four cross-country moves of my own. Since settling in Prescott in 2014, I’ve enjoyed exploring our beautiful hiking trails and practicing hot yoga when I’m not helping others simplify their space.
