How It Works


1. Schedule an Assessment


Start with a $50 in-home assessment (1–1.5 hours). We’ll tour your space, discuss your goals, and provide a rough estimate of the time needed.

You’ll also recieve helpful tips to get started right away. Payment is due at the end of the visit.

2. Book a Work Session


Call us at (928)-350-5115 or send an email to prescottorganizing@gmail.com to schedule a work session. Work sessions are scheduled in 4-hour block minimums.

You can work side by side with us to sort, declutter, and organize your space or we can work independently with intermittent input from you. The last 20 minutes are reserved for clean-up, reviewing progress, and planning next steps.

Pricing

  • $50 per hour
  • 4-hour minimum per session
  • Estimated Time by Space
    • Closet / Pantry: 4–8 hours
    • Bedroom / Playroom: 8–12 hours
    • Kitchen: 10–14 hours
    • Garage: 8–12 hours
    • Office: 16–24 hours
